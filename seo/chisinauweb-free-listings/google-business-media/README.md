# Google Business Profile media

These assets were created specifically for ChisinauWeb's verified Google Business Profile. They are brand graphics only: they do not depict a storefront, office, team, customer, client work, award, or other real-world claim.

## Final assets

- `chisinauweb-google-logo.png` — 1254×1254 square `CW` monogram, uploaded and saved as the profile logo.
- `chisinauweb-google-cover.png` — 1672×941 wide brand cover, uploaded and currently awaiting Google moderation.

## Generation mode and prompts

Mode: built-in OpenAI ImageGen, new image generation from text prompts.

Logo prompt:

> Create a polished square brand logo image for a solo web developer business named “ChisinauWeb”. Exact visible lettering: “CW” only, with the C and W clearly legible and correctly spelled as two capital Latin letters. Minimal modern vector-style monogram, premium but approachable, white lettering on a rich purple-to-deep-navy gradient using colors close to #5A27D3 and #0B1739. Strong geometric construction, generous safe margins, crisp edges, centered composition, no shadows that reduce legibility. No other text, no slogan, no mockup, no storefront, no office, no people, no devices, no client logos, no stock symbols, no watermark. Suitable as a Google Business Profile logo at small sizes.

Cover prompt:

> Create a premium wide 16:9 Google Business Profile cover for the solo web-development brand ChisinauWeb. Use a clean editorial brand-graphic composition, not a photograph and not a mock storefront. Deep navy background with controlled purple (#5A27D3) and cyan highlights, subtle geometric browser-grid motifs and abstract code/layout lines, generous negative space, sharp modern design. Include only this exact visible text, correctly spelled and with proper diacritics: large title “ChisinauWeb”; subtitle “Web design • Development • E-commerce”; small line “Solo developer · Chișinău & Moldova”. No URL, no price, no call-to-action, no awards, no client logos, no fake statistics, no people, no office, no laptop mockup, no storefront, no watermark, and no other words. Keep all text inside a central safe area so it remains legible when cropped.

The website's older downloadable graphics were inspected but not uploaded because they still used the MoldovaWebsite branding.
